# Research Question — LOCKED

> **How do transformer-based models compare with convolutional/recurrent
> deep-learning approaches for EEG-based emotion recognition, in terms of
> datasets, architectures, evaluation metrics, and reported performance —
> and where do published studies disagree?**

## Scope

**Corpus:** arXiv papers only.

**Unit of comparison:** a reported experimental result or claim from an
arXiv paper concerning EEG-based emotion recognition.

**Primary comparison dimensions:**

1. Approaches
2. Datasets
3. Architectures
4. Metrics
5. Reported performance
6. Disagreements / contradictions

## Explicit Scope Boundary

The system should **not** attempt to determine which approach is
universally "best." It should synthesize what the retrieved literature
reports and identify where comparisons are, or are not, directly valid.
